\section{Problem and method}
Real fully--symmetric quartic couplings $\lambda\in\mathrm{Sym}^4(\mathbb R^N)$, $n_{\mathrm{vars}}=\binom{N+3}{4}$. One--loop flow $\dot\lambda_{ijkl}=-\lambda_{ijkl}+(\lambda_{ijmn}\lambda_{mnkl}+\lambda_{ikmn}\lambda_{mnjl}+\lambda_{ilmn}\lambda_{mnjk})$; density matrix $\rho_{ij}=\lambda_{iklm}\lambda_{jklm}$; order parameter $T_{ij}=\rho_{ij}-\tfrac1N(\mathrm{tr}\,\rho)\delta_{ij}$. The invariant entropy--maximal locus is the Lie chain
\[ T=\mathcal L_\beta T=\mathcal L_\beta^2 T=\mathcal L_\beta^3 T=0,\qquad \mathcal L_\beta^k T=k!\,[t^k]T(\lambda(t)). \]
Solved by multistart Levenberg--Marquardt with Taylor--mode residuals and a complex--step Jacobian. Local manifold dim $=n_{\mathrm{vars}}-\mathrm{rank}\,J$ (gap--based rank); moduli $=$ dim $-$ orbit dim, where orbit dim $=\mathrm{rank}$ of the $\mathfrak{so}(N)$ rotation tangents ($\dim O(N)=\binom N2$).
